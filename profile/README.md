## What Fiatsend is

Fiatsend is payment infrastructure for businesses paying people across Africa. A business sends a payout request; Fiatsend locks the conversion, applies compliance controls, and delivers funds to the recipient's Fiatsend wallet. Recipients can keep the funds in their wallet or settle to a local rail such as mobile money.

A recipient who is new to Fiatsend receives a secure link to finish onboarding and claim the payout, so a payment is never lost because an account did not exist yet.

> **Live in Ghana today.** Settlement methods, supported assets and limits vary by corridor and account, so please confirm availability with us before you launch.

### Inside the Console

- **Collect with payment links and QR codes.** Customers pay in USDC on Stellar, and payments are confirmed on-chain automatically.
- **Fund your balance** with USDC on Stellar, Polygon or BNB Chain, with an email or WhatsApp confirmation when a deposit lands.
- **Convert stablecoins to GHS** inside your account.
- **Pay out one person or thousands.** Single and bulk payouts, including to mobile-money recipients, with on-chain references and settlement status.
- **Run it as a team.** Roles and permissions, API keys, webhooks, and separate sandbox and production environments.

## How a payout works

1. A business creates a payout in the Console or through the API.
2. Fiatsend validates the request, locks the applicable conversion, and runs the required compliance controls.
3. Funds arrive in the recipient's Fiatsend wallet, or settle through the configured Stellar or local rail.
4. The recipient holds the balance or uses an available settlement option, including automatic settlement where enabled.

## Who uses it

- **Payroll and employer-of-record platforms** paying contractors and employees without building local payout operations.
- **Marketplaces and gig platforms** paying sellers, drivers and service providers when work is complete.
- **Exchanges and wallets** offering eligible customers a compliant route from stablecoin value to local settlement.

## Security and compliance

Compliance and risk controls are part of every payment flow. Businesses are verified during onboarding, and recipients may be asked to verify their identity before using certain wallet or settlement features.

We will never ask you for a password, one-time code or recovery phrase. If you suspect fraud or a compromised account, contact us right away through the app or your business support route.

Fiatsend is a payment technology company, not a bank. Funds held in a Fiatsend wallet are not bank deposits and are not covered by government deposit insurance.
